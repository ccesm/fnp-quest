LESSON 4 · GI

GERD & Acid Disorders

Goal: Understand pathophysiology, typical symptoms, alarm features, and first-line treatment.

🧠 Pathophysiology

GERD results from reflux of gastric contents into the esophagus. Repeated exposure can irritate the mucosa and produce heartburn and regurgitation.

🔑 Clinical Pearls

  • Typical symptoms: heartburn and regurgitation.
  • Symptoms may worsen after meals or when supine.
  • Dysphagia, odynophagia, GI bleeding, weight loss, anemia, or persistent vomiting are alarm features.

💊 Treatment

Lifestyle measures and acid suppression are central. PPIs are generally more effective than H2 blockers for healing erosive esophagitis.
